# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We welcome people of every background and experience level. Participate with respect, assume good intent while checking impact, and keep feedback focused on the work.

Helpful participation includes:

- explaining decisions and disagreement with concrete evidence;
- giving people room to ask basic questions and correct mistakes;
- respecting privacy, boundaries, attribution, and informed consent;
- accepting moderator decisions and repairing harm when possible.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, sustained disruption, or publishing another person's private information without permission.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, and other spaces represented as part of the project. It also applies when someone publicly represents the project or its community.

## Reporting and enforcement

For behavior on GitHub, use GitHub's reporting and blocking tools when immediate platform action is appropriate. Project-specific concerns can be sent to the maintainer through the public contact method on the [`iumi-in` profile](https://github.com/iumi-in). Do not put private security details in a public conduct report; follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

Maintainers will review reports in good faith, limit disclosure to what is needed for resolution, and may edit or remove content, reject contributions, issue warnings, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Enforcement will consider severity, impact, history, and willingness to repair harm.

Retaliation against someone who raises a good-faith concern is itself a violation of this policy.
